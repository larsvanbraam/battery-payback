Specs used

How it's calculated

For every hour of the year, solar power you would have sent to the grid charges the battery instead (up to its charge power), and the battery then powers your home (up to its discharge power) until it's empty. Part of the energy is lost on the way, at the efficiency that matches that hour's power.

  • Hourly data hides short spikes above the battery's power (a kettle, the oven), so real savings are likely 5–10% lower.
  • Batteries Tweakers hasn't tested at low power use the average drop of the ones it has.
  • Every battery gets the same lifetime. Check the warranty: a cheap battery that fails early never earns itself back.
  • The supplier presets are one-year fixed contracts for new customers, as offered when checked (the date and source are under the picker). Your own contract may differ: enter its prices above.
  • Grid prices include VAT and the 2026 energy tax; the 2027 rate isn't published yet, so the real 2027 price will move with it. Three-year contracts are left out because no 2027 feed-in terms for them were published, and dynamic contracts aren't modelled.
  • Dynamic contracts, where a battery also charges on cheap hours, aren't modelled. They would favour bigger batteries.
